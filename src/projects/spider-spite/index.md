You are a spider. You have chosen the worst possible place to take a stroll.

A browser-based survival game rendered in isometric pseudo-3D: you navigate across a sleeping human who is large, while you are small, and who occasionally swats. You do not want to be where the hand lands. No build step, no dependencies, no regrets.

Play at **[spiderspite.com](https://spiderspite.com)**.
