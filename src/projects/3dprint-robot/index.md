A 3D-printable cycloidal robot arm, designed for **low backlash**, **commodity parts** (NEMA17 steppers, 608 bearings, magnets), and **fast iteration** on a home printer.

The whole arm is authored parametrically, with simulation and renders alongside the printable geometry — cycloidal reduction gets you high ratios and stiffness without the backlash of planetary gear trains, which is exactly what a desktop-printed arm needs to be useful.
