A parametric, addressable RGB LED panel on **flexible** PCB — designed to replace fiddly LED *strips* with flat, grid-registered tiles that line up perfectly, and to know exactly what they cost *before* committing a cent.

The baseline panel is 8×32 = 256 pixels, 160 × 40 mm, 5 mm pitch, 2-layer flex, WS2812B-2020-class integrated pixels. Every dimension is a parameter — change rows, columns, pitch, or LED and re-price instantly against a JLCPCB cost model validated to within 3.4% of a real quote. Includes tscircuit 3D PCBA views of the actual board.
