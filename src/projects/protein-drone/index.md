A browser-based real-time granular synthesizer that turns proteins into music: a drone field of grains driven by per-residue chemistry, while autonomous walkers traverse the protein's 3D contact graph and excite sustained harmonic partials of a tonal root.

The aim is musical playability over physics accuracy, but the structural detail — sequence, secondary structure, contact graph, pseudo-mode collective motion — is real, and it's what shapes the sound. Inspired by [MIT's work on protein sonification](https://news.mit.edu/2019/translating-proteins-music-0626).
