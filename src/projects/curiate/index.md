A social network for creators — musicians, visual artists, and designers — that prioritizes true fan connections over viral reach. Curiate helps creators build sustainable income through direct fan relationships and built-in monetization, without depending on algorithmic luck.

It's the most product-grade thing in the workshop: a Flutter client over a Go backend, with an architecture-decision-record trail documenting the hard calls. Follow the build in the devlog.
